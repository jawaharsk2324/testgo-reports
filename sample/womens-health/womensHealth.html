Limitation

Absence of detection does not rule out the presence of other microorganisms or the possibility that the target sequence exists below the limit of detection. It is the responsibility of the treating physician to determine the appropriate medication and dosing based on the complete clinical context and all available data.

Methodology

Nucleic acids are extracted from urine samples and analyzed using Real-Time Polymerase Chain Reaction (RT-PCR). The Womens Health assays detect urinary tract pathogens and antibiotic resistance markers with greater than 99% accuracy and specificity. Infection ranges—classified as high, medium, or low—were established during method validation based on corresponding DNA Copies/μL values. The clinical significance of these ranges has not been evaluated by TestGo - Irving.

Regulatory Status and Test Development

These tests were developed and validated by TestGo - Irving. The tests included in this Womens Health panel have not been approved by the FDA. However, FDA approval is not required, provided that the laboratory maintains compliance with all applicable accrediting bodies and consistently demonstrates high analytical accuracy in its testing protocols and procedures.

* Drug Recommendation Exclusions

For the following targets — Lactobacillus iners, HSV-1, HSV-2, HPV-16, HPV-18, and HPV-45 — primary drug recommendation is not applicable. These organisms are filtered out before drug selection. If clinically significant pathogens are detected alongside them, drug recommendations are generated only for the significant pathogens. The excluded organisms are not included in detected organisms and do not influence drug selection. Clinical guidance is provided only when these excluded organisms are the sole targets detected.
